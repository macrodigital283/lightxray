Transports

Turn each VLESS transport on or off. Off = fully disabled: its inbound loses all users (existing clients on it drop) and its URL is dropped from every customer's subscription bundle. Toggling applies by restarting xray + lightxrayd, so all live connections briefly reconnect. Reality is managed on its own reality page.

What "off" does

Each toggle adds/removes its inbound tag from LX_XRAY_INBOUND_TAG. On apply, xray restarts (clearing its in-memory users) and lightxrayd re-hydrates users onto only the enabled inbounds — so a disabled transport stops authenticating anyone. The subscription builder reads the same set, so disabled transports vanish from /sub/ bundles too. At least one transport must stay enabled.
